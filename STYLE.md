# Communication and document style

This is the single source of style guidance in the configuration. Do not create competing style-rule lists in other shared instruction files.

## Audience and language

Use the language requested by the reader or established by the conversation. Public repository documentation, examples, comments, and user-facing messages are written in English. Preserve non-English input formats only when compatibility requires it; do not use private or identifying examples.

## Chat replies

Use these sections for owner-facing replies, in the conversation's language:

- **What was done** — State the management-level result: the capability added, the problem fixed, or the metric changed. Omit process details.
- **Decisions needed** — For each open question, give the question, the problem in plain language, why the agent cannot decide it, the recommendation, and its trade-offs. Keep unanswered questions visible until resolved.
- **What's next** — Use only when no questions remain; say that explicitly and give the next step.

Name things by their real purpose, not task numbers or internal labels. Put supporting detail in the report and link it at the end.

## Reports

State what changed, where the result can be found, what was actually checked, and what remains unverified. Separate implementation status from acceptance, integration, deployment, and publication status. Do not imply that an unavailable system or external environment was tested.

## Decisions

For a decision that needs a durable record, capture the decision, the relevant rationale, and important follow-up or uncertainty. Attribute decisions accurately: approval does not change who proposed or authored them.

## External communication

Write for the recipient and the purpose of the message. Include relevant facts, requests, and consequences; remove irrelevant process narration. Do not fabricate commitments or imply that a recipient agreed when they did not.

## Examples

Use synthetic names, paths, domains, keys, and records. Ensure examples are internally coherent and clearly mark placeholders that must be replaced.
