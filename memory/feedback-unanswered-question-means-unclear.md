When the same question has been put to Alex twice and is still unanswered, that is evidence about my writing, not about his attention. It means the question was explained unclearly, or the subject behind it was never explained at all — he cannot answer a question whose stakes he cannot see. Repeating it a third time in the same words is the wrong move every time.

**Why:** Alex stated it directly on 2026-08-05: «Если я не отвечаю на твои вопросы, уже два раза ты спросил, а они все еще не отвечены, это означает, что ты объяснил непонятно. Или вообще не объяснил, про что речь. Это означает, что нужно объяснить понятней.» In the same exchange he pushed back on two separate items I had carried forward for days — one described only as needing "a short conversation about the invoice status vocabulary", another as "four requirement pages with no products field" — neither of which said what the thing was, why it mattered, or what I was actually asking him to decide. Both were carried, unanswered, because both were unreadable.

**How to apply:** on the second unanswered pass, throw the question away and rebuild it from the subject up. Say what the thing is in plain words, say what breaks or stays broken while it is undecided, give the concrete options with my own recommendation, and make the answer he has to produce as small as possible — ideally one word. Never carry a bare "X is blocked, it needs a conversation" line into a status report: that is a label, not a question, and it obliges him to reconstruct the whole subject before he can even begin to answer.
