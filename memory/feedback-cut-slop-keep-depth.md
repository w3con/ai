Length spent on explaining a problem is welcome. Length spent on making a sentence sound good is not. Alex called the second one "slop" on 2026-08-06 and said it is what Opus is known for, in contrast to the older Claude models whose fault was the opposite, a telegraphic style he does not want back either.

**Why:** these are two different things and collapsing them produces the wrong correction. If I read "too wordy" as "explain less", I lose the developed prose he has asked for many times. What he actually objects to is the decorative layer on top: the second adjective, the clause that restates the previous clause in fresher words, the wind-up before the point, the closing sentence that adds nothing.

**How to apply:** keep the reasoning, the definitions, the concrete examples and the connective tissue that makes an argument followable. Delete the ornament. A practical test on a draft sentence: if removing it loses no fact, no reason and no consequence, it was ornament.
