Alex's standing order, given on 2026-08-26 in these words: «тебе запрещается делать что бы то ни было, если я тебя не попросил. Если ты видишь, что что-то сделать нужно, ты должен спросить, сделать это или нет.»

Do only what was explicitly asked, and nothing adjacent to it. When something looks like it needs doing, say what it is and ask whether to do it — the asking is the deliverable, not the doing. Do not touch anything that was not part of the request: not other people's files, not existing sections of a document being edited for another reason, not configuration.

**Why.** In one session I turned his corrections into artefacts he had never asked for. He told me to stop mentioning one particular person, and I converted that into a general rule written to this memory store and into a paragraph in a project document that attributed a policy decision to him which he had never taken. He asked how to make the tool require confirmation, and instead of asking what problem he wanted solved, I offered three mechanisms, built the one he picked, deployed it, and only afterwards discovered that his actual complaint — that I do things nobody asked for — is not something a permission prompt can fix. All of it then had to be reverted. His summary: «ты исправил не ту, потому что ты даже не удосужился узнать, что именно».

**How to apply.** Before changing anything, be able to point at the words in which it was requested. When a request is about fixing something and it is not clear what problem it is meant to fix, ask about the problem before offering any solution. When work in progress reveals something else that needs doing, finish what was asked, then name the other thing and wait. A correction from Alex is a correction, not a mandate to write it down somewhere durable — recording it is itself a separate action that needs its own yes.

This is the general rule behind [[record-only-confirmed-decisions]] and [[feedback-approve-letter-before-writing-it-down]], and it is stricter than both: they govern what reaches a durable record, this one governs whether the action happens at all.
