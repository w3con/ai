Read `kb/BizDev/letter-style.md` before drafting any letter to a real person. It was derived on 2026-08-24 from Alexander's own rewrite of a drafted letter, which cut a third of the words without losing anything, and it is the canonical version: every rule there carries a wrong example and a right one, which is the only form in which these rules are usable. The core, so that a session which never opens the file still holds it:

Say the thing, never announce that you are about to say it: a sentence whose only job is to introduce the next sentences («two things are worth a minute of your time», «let me explain why») is deleted. Build a chain of consequences, not a list of true facts and not a proof: a letter does not argue a case to a judge, it puts an idea in front of somebody and shows what follows from it for them, so each sentence should be caused by the one above it. Do not narrate your own conduct or intentions («I am not going to chase her»), though one short judgement that tells the reader how to read a fact may stay. Do not explain to the reader what the reader knows better than you. Do not answer an objection nobody raised, which is the writing-side face of [[feedback-no-saccharine-in-french-letters]]. Frame the request as the means to something the reader is already trying to achieve. Offer the transferable skill, not only the finished artefact. Keep courtesy present but never ostentatious: high style, elaborate gratitude and explanation piled on explanation read as stiffness or as softening the ground before a large ask, and they produce puzzlement, suspicion and irritation rather than warmth, so directness and plain clarity rank above all of it.

The generating test behind all of it: every sentence must carry a fact, a request, or a consequence of the sentence before it, and a sentence carrying none of the three is deleted. Length is the result of that test, never the target.

**Why:** on 2026-08-24 Alexander rewrote a letter I had drafted, cutting it by a third, and then asked for the rules behind his edit. My first attempt at writing them down was rejected for a specific reason worth keeping: I stated each rule abstractly with no examples, and a rule stated that way is unusable by a session reading it cold. Every rule now carries a wrong version and a right version. He also struck one rule I had invented, about always quoting the smallest true number, as advice that does not generalise beyond that one letter.
