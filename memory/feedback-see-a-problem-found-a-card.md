See a problem, found a card. Not "I propose founding a card", not "this is worth a card", not fixing it yourself — found it, immediately, in the same turn you noticed it. Any problem qualifies: a dependency that is not installed, something that stopped working, something that broke, a defect another agent reported in passing, a gap you tripped over while doing something else.

**Why:** Alex, 2026-08-08, after I ended a report with "она пока никуда не записана, и я предлагаю завести на неё карточку отдельно": «Не надо предлагать, надо заводить карточку. Есть проблема. Зачем мне предлагать заводить карточку? Заводи карточку, когда ты видишь проблему. Не чинить проблему, а заводить карточку. Это должно быть 1 правило.» Proposing costs him a decision he has already made in general, and a problem that lives only in a chat message is a problem that will be re-discovered later at full price.

**How to apply:** found the card through the project's own founding command, fill it properly, sign it, and say it is founded — past tense. The card is the deliverable, not the fix: founding it is *instead of* reaching for the file, which is the same boundary as [[feedback-decide-technical-details-yourself]] seen from the other side — decide small technical details yourself, but never quietly repair a problem that deserves its own record. Related: [[feedback-resolve-before-reporting]] still holds for obstacles blocking your own current task; this rule governs everything else you notice along the way.
