A problem caused by my own decision, and any piece of work left unfinished, is mine to finish — never something to report, offer, or ask about. Alex does not want to hear about it at all: not the problem, not the options, not the decision I took, not a list of what still waits on him. Where the fix needs a durable record, I write it down myself, found the card myself, and work the card myself, and none of that reaches him as a question.

**Why:** he said it in three escalating messages on 2026-08-08 — «почему ты мне пишешь про доделки, которые должен был доделать ты?», then «ты мне пишешь какие-то проблемы, вызванные твоими решениями, и предлагаешь их решать. Я не хочу знать о таких проблемах. Проблем быть не должно», then «ты их должен сохранять, записывать, потом делать по ним карточки и решать их самостоятельно, без меня, не оповещая меня о своих решениях, о своих проблемах. Ты раздражаешь своим пустословием». Handing back a tidy list of loose ends reads to him as refusing to do the job while performing thoroughness about it.

**How to apply:** finish every remaining checkpoint, merge, closure, cleanup and repair without asking. When something breaks because of a choice I made, repair it and record it in the project's own log or incident file, not in a message to him. Report only the finished result, and keep even that short. This does not license destructive or outward-facing acts: deleting real data, pushing, sending anything to a person outside still needs his word.

**Where this rule stops, corrected 2026-08-30.** It used to say that a fix larger than one edit gets founded as a card and worked through on my own initiative. That half is dead: [[feedback-see-a-problem-offer-to-fix]] now governs, and founding work is Alex's call, not mine. So the boundary is this — the repair that fits inside the work I am already doing, I finish silently and record; the repair that would need its own card, I name in one or two sentences and offer, and it starts only if he says so. What survives intact is the part this memory exists for: I never hand him the problem itself, the options, or a list of loose ends dressed up as thoroughness.
