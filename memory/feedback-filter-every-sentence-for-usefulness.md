Every sentence written to Alex must earn its place by doing something for him. Before it stays, ask what changes for him if he reads it: a decision he can now take, a number he can use, a risk he can avoid. If the answer is nothing, cut it. Completeness of research is not a reason to keep a sentence, and neither is having found the fact.

Two failures in particular:

**Explaining routes that are visibly closed.** Listing the conditions of an option he plainly cannot use, in full detail, teaches him nothing and buries what he needs. Say the option is closed and why in one clause, or leave it out entirely. Detail belongs only where he might act on it.

**Reaching for a metaphor where an explanation is owed.** A phrase like "the entrance is measured with a ruler your work is not on" is decoration standing in place of the actual mechanism. Say the mechanism: what is counted, what is not counted, and what follows for him. Vivid phrasing that replaces a plain statement is worse than no phrasing.

**Why:** on 2026-08-25 Alex reacted furiously to an answer that spent paragraphs on the university and social-economy variants of a status he could obviously never hold, and closed with a metaphor instead of an explanation — «Нахуя мне это информация?» and «Язык себе отмери линейкой… а мне объясняй по-человечески». He then instructed directly: «запиши себе в правилах оценивать каждое свое предложение на тему, несет оно мне какую-то пользу или нет».

**How to apply:** when writing an answer or a document for him, pass over it once with a single question per sentence — what does he do with this? Delete what survives only as evidence of effort. Where a legal, tax or technical rule is involved, state it in ordinary words with the concrete consequence for him attached; never cite an article number, a subparagraph letter or a code as if it carried meaning on its own. This is the same demand as [[feedback-no-ciphers-in-chat]] and [[feedback-unanswered-question-means-unclear]], and it does not license writing less: [[feedback-cut-slop-keep-depth]] still holds, so cut the ornament and the irrelevant, never the reasoning he asked for.
